We’re building a hyperscaler for company-owned intelligence.

Our work spans RL environments, evaluation, post-training, and inference. The goal is models that learn from real work, with weights, data, and training recipes their owners control.

Thesis

Intelligence is an arms race. The market for better data and environments is not zero-sum: internal research and outside specialization can both add capability. We’re building to deliver improvements worth more than the research time required to reproduce them.
